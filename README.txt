PRAMOD & SEERA — WEDDING INVITATION WEBSITE
===========================================

FILES
- index.html       Main invitation page
- style.css        Design and mobile-responsive layout
- script.js        English/Nepali toggle and wedding countdown
- thank-you.html   Confirmation page after RSVP submission

PUBLISH FREE ON NETLIFY (NO CODING REQUIRED)
1. Extract the ZIP file on your computer so you can see the folder named pramod-seera-wedding-site.
2. Visit https://app.netlify.com/drop and sign in or create a free Netlify account.
3. Drag the extracted folder (the folder containing index.html) onto the Netlify Drop upload area.
4. Netlify will publish the site and give you a public address ending in netlify.app. You can change the site name in Site configuration / Domain management.
5. Open the public link on your phone and test the language switch and RSVP form.

RSVP FORM SETUP
- The form is configured for Netlify Forms (data-netlify="true").
- After the first deployment, open your site in the Netlify dashboard and check Forms / Forms submissions (the dashboard label may vary).
- If Netlify asks you to enable form detection, enable it and redeploy the site. Submit one test RSVP to confirm submissions are being collected.
- Guest responses will be visible in your Netlify site dashboard. Netlify's free plan limits can change, so check the current plan limits if you expect many submissions.
- Do not collect sensitive information in the RSVP form.

CUSTOMISE BEFORE SHARING WIDELY
- Exact venue address, ceremony start time and programme schedule are placeholders because they were not supplied.
- Add the exact venue and a Google Maps link once confirmed. The current page says Jhapa, Nepal and does not invent a specific venue.
- The countdown uses Nepal time (UTC+05:45) and the wedding date 16 February 2027.
- The RSVP form currently asks for guest name, contact, attendance, guest count and an optional message.

SHARE
Copy the Netlify URL and send it through WhatsApp, Messenger, email or print it as a QR code. The page includes basic social preview metadata.

NOTES
- Hosting on Netlify can be free on its current free plan; plan limits and dashboard labels can change.
- A custom domain is optional and usually costs extra. The free netlify.app address is enough to share the invitation.
